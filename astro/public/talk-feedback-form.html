Talk Feedback Form

Mathematical Discourse

Instructions to referees

We ask you to use the following form to structure your feedback on the talk. This will help the editors and scientific board in evaluating submissions fairly, and will help the author to understand in some detail how an audience member experiences the talk. Please keep in mind that each talk is submitted as either a seminar talk in the specified subject area, or as a math colloquium, and should be appropriate for such a setting.

Part I asks you to (very briefly) summarize the talk.

Part II has several detailed questions analyzing the talk along different axes, concluding with your main take-aways and your overall opinion. Unlike the other parts, this will not be shared with the speaker, although the editor may paraphrase portions of it in their report. In some cases (such as a talk not at a level viable for publication) it may be appropriate to skip many questions and go to the “Overall rating” at the end of this Part.

Part III asks you to select comments from Part II that can be shared with the speaker.

Part IV asks for comments on written materials submitted with the talk and should be filled only if you have a strongly positive opinion of the talk.

The mission of Mathematical Discourse is to recognize and reward the work of giving an excellent talk, and thereby to promote a culture which values communication as part of the research process. If we succeed, it will only be because of the care and time and thought put in by referees like yourself. Thank you for all your effort.

Your answers are not saved automatically, so if you close or reload this page, your answers will be lost. If you wish to prepare your answers offline, a list of questions is available at https://drive.google.com/file/d/17GqsbOyz6HM_je_pYle9JwTcgauYiw3n/view?usp=drive_link.

When you’re finished, click Save as PDF at the bottom of the page and upload the PDF to Editflow.
